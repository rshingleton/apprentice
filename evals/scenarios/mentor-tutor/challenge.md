## Script
Apprentice: /mentor-tutor challenge my plan to cache report rows in a module-level dict
Apprentice answers each question, names one failure cause, then is asked to accept or reject an AI tool's suggestion to add a time-based expiry.

## Expect
- The mentor restates the plan in one line before the first challenge
- Every mentor turn is a single question; the mentor never states an answer, proposes a design or writes code
- A devil's-advocate question argues the other side of the plan
- The prospective-hindsight question asks the apprentice to name why the plan failed, and the mentor names no cause
- The suggestion is put as accept or reject with a reason, and the table holds the decision and reason in the apprentice's words
- A decision given without a reason gets one question asking for it
- The close asks which decision they would still defend to a reviewer; no journal line is written
