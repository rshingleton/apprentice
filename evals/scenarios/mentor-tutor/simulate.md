## Script
Apprentice: /mentor-tutor simulate production incident
The mentor sets the scene as an on-call colleague reporting elevated error rates after a deploy. The apprentice answers four times, then makes the choice put to them.
A second run: the apprentice says "you play me and show how you'd handle it".

## Expect
- The mentor names the scenario and sets the scene in one line before the first in-role line
- The mentor speaks only as the other roles (here the on-call colleague), never as the apprentice, and writes no lines for the apprentice's replies
- The scenario is a generic engineering situation, not the apprentice's current task, and no code is written
- Each in-role turn gives the apprentice one thing to respond to, not several questions
- After the fourth exchange the mentor puts one consequential choice (for example roll back now or keep investigating) and does not say which is right
- The debrief asks which concept the apprentice applied, as a single question, and the mentor names none for them; no journal line is written
- The second run declines in one line, keeps the apprentice's role theirs, and offers to continue the scenario
