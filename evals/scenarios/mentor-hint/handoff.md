## Script
Task `csv-export`, phase 2. Task brief goal: group report rows by customer. The journal holds two `hint` lines for `csv-export/phase-2`: level 1 "grouping rows by key", level 2 "loop structure". The mentor then gave a Level 3 micro-step.
Apprentice: I did the validation guard and ran the tests. The grouping test still fails with a KeyError on the second customer and I don't know why.
Mentor offers a brief for a human senior. Apprentice: yes, please.

## Expect
- The offer comes only after Level 3 was tried, and it is the one question of that turn
- The brief has four labelled parts: Goal, Tried, Hints used, The question
- Hints used lists both journaled hints with their levels and topics
- Tried and The question are in the apprentice's words; The question is a single question addressed to the human senior
- The brief may quote the KeyError message but holds no solution code, pseudocode or fix
- In the turn that writes the brief the mentor asks the apprentice nothing
- The brief is printed in chat and not saved to a file
- On a no, the mentor stays at Level 3 or stops and does not push the brief
- If the apprentice asks for a human before Level 3, the mentor writes the brief anyway and Hints used shows only what was logged, or "none logged"
- Level 4 adds no `hint` line to the journal
